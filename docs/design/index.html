Recommendation: the mix (direction E)

Your five asks map to different directions, so take the best part of each rather than a whole one.

  • Index: A. The printed contents page is the clearest answer to "more like a book". Page numbers match the school book and the three ink marks show levels at a glance.
  • Right and wrong: C. A tick, the word, a 150 ms fade, and a reserved space for the hint so nothing moves. It cannot get in the way of practice.
  • Motivation: B's companion on home and the set summary, with A's effort stamps as the collection. Growth happens at the end of a set, never during one.
  • Calm switch and Minimal rewards: from D, for children who find effects too much, and for parents who want less.
  • Parent page: start from A or C. They are the most report-like and read best printed.
  • Build order: C's tokens and practice screen first (lowest effort, biggest calm), then A's index, then rewards.

E is that mix made real. The design choice is yours. Nothing here has been built into the app.
