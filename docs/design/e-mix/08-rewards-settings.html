Rewards and settings

Choose how much reward each child sees. These are the child’s own settings, so Mira and Kabir can differ.

Settings for

Design intent These settings are not stored in the app yet. Here they change this prototype for this visit and carry to the other pages.

Reward level

Calm mode

No movement, no bright fills. Pip and stamps become plain paper tones and ticks. Your child also has a Calm switch at the top of every page.

Sound

Off by default. When on, a right answer plays one very soft tone. Nothing plays while your child is thinking.

Daily goal

Minutes of practice that fill the day. A short day is fine, a missed day never breaks the streak, and a rest-day token covers a day off.

What Mira sees

Home

Grows after each finished set.

Set finished. 7 of 8 right. 6 minutes.

Preview popups

Popups only appear when a set ends, last about 3 seconds and can be skipped. Pick a mode, then play one. This preview does not change the saved settings above.
