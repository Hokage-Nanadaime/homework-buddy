How this avoids overwhelm

  • Home shows one next step. The full map opens only when you ask for it, and it can always be read as a plain list.
  • While you work, Sprig is small and still. A tiny nod or a kind head tilt appears at the answer, never on the whole screen.
  • The big celebration comes once, after a set. A parent can turn rewards down to quiet or minimal.
