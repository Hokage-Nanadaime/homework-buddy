# Sample content

The real book content (lessons, exercises, question banks, Maths units, the India outline map) is private and is not in this repository.
To let the app build and its tests run, this tree ships a few small things written for it:

- a sample Maths unit (`maths/sample/unit.ts`): adding and comparing numbers, with answers computed by code;
- a sample reading lesson (`english/sample/lesson.ts`): a made-up poem and a made-up garden story with every kind of question; it also stands in for the EVS book;
- a placeholder in place of the India map.

None of it comes from a school book. To add a real book, follow `docs/BOOK_PIPELINE.md` with your own legally obtained material, and keep it private.
